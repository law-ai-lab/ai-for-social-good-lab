# Store Journey Barrier Map #001

**2026-10-16 · v0.1 · Desk-research map, not an observed participant journey.**

Scope: community-dwelling adults approximately 70+ who want to choose and buy groceries and have difficulty making reliable store round trips. All listed barriers are **H: possible barriers**, unless explicitly identified as a sourced finding. **E** denotes source-reported evidence, **U** unknown, and **D** a research decision. Alternatives are categories to check locally, not availability promises.

## Provisional priority D001

Investigate **completed purchases → boarding suitable return transport with bags**. This makes a possible round-trip failure testable without assuming delivery is best. It is not a user-validated ranking or an assertion that a named service fails.

The next [30–60-minute validation task](../problem-discovery-001.md#next-validation-step--3060-minutes) is a public-document audit of one locality. Record successful arrangements too. If outbound travel, payment, or the final home transfer appears more consequential, revise the priority.

## Cross-cutting safeguards

Choice belongs to the adult; chosen assistance can preserve autonomy. Do not equate age, disability, lack of a smartphone, or use of help with inability to decide. Provide non-digital and non-voice participation paths. No participant journey, exact home, health detail, payment credential, or contact information belongs in this public map.
