# Mission #001 — Source Register

**Checked:** 2026-10-16 · **Scope:** focused Japanese desk review, not exhaustive or systematic.

We began with the five owner-specified URLs, followed relevant government links, and located the specified paper's indexed abstract and full-text search extract. We prioritized original government publications and research. No interviews or independent service evaluation were conducted. Search/retrieval can omit material; an accessible government case report is not proof of current operational availability. Dates below distinguish publication, study period, and access.

**E:** a source reports a finding; **H:** hypothesis; **U:** unknown; **D:** provisional decision. Evidence strengths below refer to the stated use, not an overall score for the organization. Links are original sources, not Lab endorsements.

## S1 — Geographic food-access indicator

- **Publisher/date:** MAFF / Policy Research Institute; release **2024-02-27**, estimates for **2020**.
- [Release and figures](https://www.maff.go.jp/j/press/kanbo/kihyo01/240227.html)
- [Food Accessibility Maps / 食料品アクセスマップ](https://www.maff.go.jp/primaff/seika/fsc/faccess/a_map.html)
- [2020 table and definitions](https://www.maff.go.jp/primaff/seika/fsc/faccess/2020_table01.html)
- **Method:** census mesh and store-location data; indicator concerns people 65+ at least 500 m in straight-line distance from a store and unable to use a car. Covered stores include supermarkets, convenience stores, and drugstores, among others.
- **E:** approximately 9.04 million people, 25.6% of the 65+ population; 5.66 million and 31.0% among 75+.
- **Strength/limits:** useful national spatial estimate, not observed inability, actual walking routes, preferences, or round-trip safety. This is not a 2026 population count. Store data/definitions differ from earlier estimates, limiting trend comparisons. Our approximately 70+ scope is different.
- **Design implication — D:** use maps for context, not individual eligibility or diagnosis.

## S2 — Municipal responses and existing policy measures

- **Publisher/date:** MAFF, FY2025 survey, report **March 2026**; fieldwork **2025-08-27–2026-01-30**.
- [Owner-specified overview](https://www.maff.go.jp/j/shokusan/eat/access_genjo.html)
- [Original report, pp. 1–3](https://www.maff.go.jp/j/shokusan/eat/attach/pdf/access_genjo-15.pdf)
- **Method:** questionnaire to 1,741 municipalities including Tokyo special wards; 1,238 valid responses (71.1%).
- **E:** 1,106 responding municipalities (89.3%) reported some need for countermeasures. Among these 1,106, 86.8% reported administrative or private measures. The report describes community transport, delivery/shopping assistance, and mobile retail.
- **Strength/limits:** strong description of responding administrations' reports; nonresponse, administrative knowledge, and self-report limit interpretation. Existence of a measure does not establish resident-level access, satisfaction, or effectiveness. Percentages are not older-adult prevalence.
- **Implication — D:** inspect local measures before proposing another service; investigate actual coverage rather than counting programs.

## S3 — Housing and living environment of older adults

- **Publisher/date:** Cabinet Office, **FY2023 survey / 2024 reporting cycle**; exact HTML release day not verified.
- [Owner-specified results, Chapter 2 §4, Q28/Q30](https://www8.cao.go.jp/kourei/ishiki/r05/html/chapter2_4.html)
- [Methods, Chapter 1](https://www8.cao.go.jp/kourei/ishiki/r05/html/chapter1.html)
- **Method:** nationwide stratified two-stage random sample of 4,000 adults 65+, excluding institutional residents; mail with online option, **2023-10-26–11-30**; 2,677 valid responses (66.9%).
- **E:** 80.4% selected nearby supermarket/shop shopping as an outing purpose. Multiple-response transport items included walking 50.1%, driving oneself 46.6%, and riding in family/others' cars 27.1%.
- **Strength/limits:** useful national descriptive context; self-report and nonresponse apply. Multiple-response percentages are not exclusive mode shares. Outing-purpose and general transport questions do not show grocery-trip mode, unmet need, or preference for stores over delivery.
- **Implication — D:** ask about a person's actual journey and chosen alternatives; do not infer preference from outing frequency.

## S4 — Peer-reviewed Japanese shopping-difficulty research

- **Citation:** Ishikawa M et al. *Food Accessibility and Perceptions of Shopping Difficulty among Elderly People Living Alone in Japan.* **2016;20(9):904–911**. DOI: **10.1007/s12603-015-0694-6**. November 2016 issue; full-text index gives online date 2016-02-08.
- [Owner-specified PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27791220/)
- [Publisher article/abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S127977072300475X)
- [Full-text archive](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12878531/)
- **Method:** 2013 postal cross-sectional survey in seven municipalities; GIS-informed sampling of older adults living alone who could shop alone; 2,028 valid respondents, 58.6% response; adjusted logistic regression.
- **E:** perceived difficulty was associated with walking/vision limitations, car use, distance, and other factors; most respondents reported shopping was easy.
- **Strength/limits:** observational associations, not causation. Selected independent shoppers, living-alone scope, nonresponse, older data, self-report, and unmeasured health/service factors restrict generalization. It does not rank journey stages or prove return-trip failure.
- **Verification limit:** abstract and indexed full-text extracts were available; direct archive retrieval encountered a browser-check page. No complete table reanalysis was performed.
- **Implication — D:** examine personal abilities and support as well as distance; seek contradictory successful journeys.

## S5 — A retailer's shopping bus / 買物バス

- **Publisher/date:** MAFF case sheet, **2025-10-14**.
- [Regional initiatives index](https://www.maff.go.jp/j/shokusan/eat/access_jirei.html)
- [Super Mall Lucky / Marushime, Yokote, Akita — original sheet](https://www.maff.go.jp/j/shokusan/eat/attach/pdf/access_jirei-17.pdf)
- **Method:** descriptive implementation case, not controlled research.
- **E:** the sheet describes free transport from local stops to the store, approximately 90 minutes for shopping, and return near users' homes.
- **Strength/limits:** verifies a documented round-trip model, not an independently measured autonomy benefit or 2026 operational timetable. Coverage, eligibility, boarding and bag help, and current terms have not been checked.
- **Implication — D:** existing store-visit support is a serious comparator; assess the return leg explicitly.

## S6 — Mobile retail / 移動販売

- **Publisher/date:** MAFF case sheet, **2024-12-03**; some operational figures refer to July 2024.
- [Co-op Sapporo mobile retail — original sheet](https://www.maff.go.jp/j/shokusan/eat/attach/pdf/access_jirei-15.pdf)
- **Method:** descriptive service case.
- **E:** describes mobile grocery retail, vehicle steps/aisle/shelf adaptations, and some customers combining it with home delivery.
- **Strength/limits:** historical provider case, not comparative evidence of preference or outcomes. No current stop, eligibility, stock, cost, or availability was verified for a particular resident.
- **Implication — D:** people may combine methods rather than choose a single replacement. A vehicle coming closer may help some people while retaining untested access barriers for others (H).

## S7 — Delivery with paper ordering: a historical counterexample

- **Publisher/date:** MAFF regional index labels this case **2011 (H23)**; the PDF includes FY2009 business information. Treat it as historical, not current terms.
- [Co-opnet / Co-op Deli delivery case, pp. 1–2](https://www.maff.go.jp/j/shokusan/eat/pdf/16njirei.pdf)
- **Method:** descriptive service account.
- **E:** describes handing a paper order form to a delivery worker as well as internet ordering.
- **Strength/limits:** establishes that non-digital ordering has been documented, not that a particular option remains available today. Do not reuse historical fees, discounts, or coverage as current advice.
- **Implication — D:** investigate delivery fairly alongside store access; lack of smartphone use alone does not establish that delivery cannot work.
