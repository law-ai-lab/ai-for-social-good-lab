# Product Vision Update #002 — Joyful Shopping
**Founder:** Rie Nakahara · **Date:** 2026-10-16 · **Status:** Founder-defined vision, not validated research evidence.

## Authority and continuity
This records the founder's October 16 Product Vision Update #002 alongside the Master Project Handoff. The December 31, 2026 Public Prototype v0.1 target remains. It updates design intent; it does not retrospectively change Mission #001's findings or demonstrate demand, usability, clinical benefit, or feasibility.

AI for Social Good Lab is independent public-interest R&D, separate from ClearJP and the professional AI Evaluation Portfolio. It is not a ClearJP product, registered organization, employment role, or operational support service.

## Purpose
Help people retain the right to live their own lives, with independence, curiosity, pleasure, connection, dignity, privacy, and choice. Older adults remain the primary research and accessibility priority; wider appeal is a possibility to investigate.

**Safety is the foundation. Joy is the purpose.**  
**Advanced inside. Simple outside.**  
**Make everyday life something to look forward to.**

Shopping can be explored as an opportunity for discovery, meals, memories, and connection, as well as access to necessities. These are founder-defined design interests; individual benefits and preferences require research. Safety must support meaningful life rather than compulsory surveillance.

## Shopping together — always optional
Explore shopping alone, with real family or friends, with an explicitly virtual fictional AI character, or alongside genuine recipes/stories/materials the person chooses. Do not rank companionship above solitude. “No thanks,” skip, pause, and exit must be ordinary choices without losing practical support.

Avoid the Japanese product name 「コンパニオン」. The earlier “お買い物サポート — Grocery Access Companion” is a superseded working label for naming purposes; no final name has been approved.

A future AI character must identify itself clearly as virtual. A scripted character demo must be labeled scripted, not represented as a functioning generative AI system. No affectionate persona may conceal uncertainty, pressure disclosure, or block grocery-access information.

Real-family/friend participation is initially an in-person or supporter-assisted possibility, not a promised app invitation, messaging, or synchronization capability.

## Authentic memories and sensory associations
Food-related recipes, images, words, smells, tastes, and experiences are research themes. A normal web prototype can present text/images and optional dialogue; it cannot produce physical smells or tastes.

Keep these categories visibly separate:
- User-provided or authorized historical material, with provenance.
- Clearly fictional demonstration recipes/stories.
- Newly generated suggestions, labeled generated rather than historical.

Never invent what a user smelled, tasted, remembered, or experienced. Never attribute generated words, wishes, opinions, or memories to a real individual. Encourage new discoveries as well as optional reminiscence; remembering the past must not be required or assumed pleasant.

Use fictional examples by default in v0.1. If temporary user input is considered later, first define processing, deletion, external transmission, and hosting behavior. “Not stored” alone is insufficient explanation. No persistent personal-memory database, private-photo uploads, recordings, or real-person likeness/voice feature in v0.1.

## Representations inspired by loved ones — later research only
A future virtual representation inspired by a living or deceased person is not that person. It must never claim the person is present, alive, or communicating through the system.

Research authorization for each material and person, informed consent of relevant living individuals, and deceased-person dignity and privacy interests. Ownership of one photograph must not be treated as permission for all likeness, voice, story, or recording uses. No legal sufficiency is established by this vision.

Preserve source provenance and separate historical content from synthetic responses. Provide pause, removal, disable, and clear explanations. Investigate distress, grief manipulation, confusion, dependency, and displacement of real relationships. Do not implement real-person or deceased-person representations for the December prototype.

## Cognitive engagement: hypothesis only
Enjoyable choice, recipe discussion, curiosity, reminiscence, and social participation are possible activities to study. Providing an activity, measuring engagement or quality of life, and demonstrating prevention of cognitive decline are separate claims.

No memory improvement, dementia prevention/treatment, diagnosis, cognitive score, medical screening, therapeutic program, or health-data collection is part of v0.1. No reward for “correct” recall or compulsory brain training. The [research backlog](../research/joy-companionship-memory-backlog-002.md) contains initial evidence notes and unresolved questions.

## Priority and exclusions
The [prototype scope](prototype-scope-v0.1.md) combines a welcoming experience with one researched grocery-access scenario. Optional entertainment must not delay useful access, obscure official information, or expand into a platform.

Keep Silver Life Exchange, Daily Care Companion, Human Knowledge Data Bank, sensory hardware, and robotics in the long-term research backlog. Essential help never depends on volunteering, credits, sharing knowledge, or data donation. AI does not replace safe transport, human care, professional oversight, emergency services, or relationships.

Mission #001 remains the foundation: [problem definition](../problem-discovery-001.md), [journey map](../research/store-journey-barrier-map-001.md), and [ethics principles](../ethics-privacy/principles.md). All possible benefits remain hypotheses. No participant research or software implementation is reported by this update.
